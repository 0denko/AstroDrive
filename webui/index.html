Step 5 of 5 — Connect Your Telescope in KStars / Ekos

AstroDrive already pre-configured LX200 OnStep to use /dev/onstep at 9600 baud — just pick the driver and click Connect:

  1. Close this panel (or go to http://astroberry.local) and open KStars → Ekos.
  2. In your Ekos profile, select Mount: LX200 OnStep (optional: add Aux: SkySafari to steer from your phone on port 9624).
  3. Click Start Ekos, then click Connect on LX200 OnStep.
  4. Bench-test off the telescope first: tap N/S/E/W slew arrows in Ekos and confirm both motors turn smoothly and quietly.
